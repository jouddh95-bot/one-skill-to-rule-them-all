# CHARGIX theme v10 · "Small room"

One miniature room (a three-quarter diorama box) is the stage for the whole home page. It opens on a
short loop that plays by itself, then the visitor's scroll plays a pre-rendered film of the same box,
forwards and backwards. Two stories share the stage:

- Story 1 (`#work`, prefix `ck`, 263 frames): a home baker, Sweet & Co. (made-up brand), from the idea
  at 6:40 PM on Day 01, through finding CHARGIX and sending the brief, overnight change to a bakery,
  baking while the store is built, to launch day (Day 14) and the first (illustrative) orders.
- Story 2 (`#nova`, prefix `nv`, 237 frames): a clothing designer, NOVA (made-up label), from late
  sketches to the press, packing, launch day (Day 30) in English and Spanish, and first orders.

The film is labelled on screen: "Rendered film, played by your scroll. Brands and orders are made up."
It is not interactive 3D. The only interactive 3D on the page is the 3D Experience package preview
(`pk-3d.glb`, model-viewer, lazy-loaded).

## Files
- layout/ theme.liquid, password.liquid: self-hosted fonts (Instrument Serif, Inter Tight,
  JetBrains Mono) from assets.
- assets/ cx.css (design system), cx.js (header, menu, reveal, lazy model-viewer, forms),
  cx-story.js (story engine), cx-demo.css (demo storefronts), `ck-*`/`nv-*` film frames
  (`-d-NNN` 1280 wide desktop, `-m-NNN` 960 wide phones, `-poster`), opening loops
  `ck-loop.{mp4,webm}` and `nv-loop.{mp4,webm}`, card images `sw-*`, `nv-*`, demo store images
  `demo-*`, package previews `pk-*`.
- sections/ story (stage + captions + interface cards + clock + chapter bar), packages (cards + matrix),
  what-we-do, steps, care, faq, closing, header, footer, demo-store, contact, onboarding, main-*.
- snippets/ story-card (phone, brief, demo store, demo orders, design picker cards).
- templates/index.json is generated by ../tools/v10-index.py (caption length is checked there).

## Story engine data contract (sections/story.liquid + assets/cx-story.js)
- Blocks keyed to film frames: caption (from/to/hold), chapter (from/to), card (from/to, place,
  points-at x,y, inner motion frames). Clock keys `[frame, minutes, day]`, camera keys
  `[frame, fx, fy, zoom]` per desktop/phone.
- Test hook: `window.__cxStory[id]` → `{progress, frame, loaded, n}`.
- No JS / reduced motion: poster, intro and every caption show as a plain sequence.

## Rebuilding the film
- Clips: Kling 5-second clips chained by shared keyframes (Higgsfield). Story configs in
  ../tools/v10-stories/*.json; `python3 tools/v10-frames.py <story.json> <out>` writes the frames.
- Loops: ../tools/v10-loops.py. Card and package images: ../tools/v10-assets.py.
